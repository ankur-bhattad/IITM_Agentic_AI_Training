# Returns Policy

## Standard return window
- Most items can be returned within 15 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.
- Proof of purchase (order ID or invoice) is required to initiate a return.
- Items returned after 15 days of delivery are not eligible for a standard return, regardless of
  reason, unless the item falls under the damaged/defective category below.

## Non-returnable categories
- Innerwear, swimwear, and personal care/hygiene items are not eligible for return once opened,
  for hygiene reasons.
- Customized or personalized products are not eligible for return unless defective.
- Gift cards and digital goods are non-returnable.

## Damaged or defective products
- If a product arrives damaged or defective, the customer has 48 hours from delivery to report it
  with photos of the damage.
- Damaged/defective reports are exempt from the "unused, original packaging" requirement and from
  the 15-day window — they follow their own 48-hour reporting window instead.
- Once a damaged/defective report is verified, the customer is offered a free replacement or a
  full refund, at their choice; a return pickup is arranged at no cost to the customer.

## How to initiate a return
- Returns are initiated through the order history page; a pickup is scheduled automatically for
  eligible returns.
- Once the returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse, the refund process begins
  (see Refunds Policy for timelines).
