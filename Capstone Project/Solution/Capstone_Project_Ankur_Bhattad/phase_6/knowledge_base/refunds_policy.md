# Refunds Policy

## Refund processing timeline
- Once a returned item is received and inspected at the warehouse, the refund is processed within
  5-7 business days.
- Refunds are not issued before the returned item is received and inspected — a refund cannot be
  processed at the time a return is merely requested.

## Refund method
- Orders paid online (card, UPI, net banking) are refunded to the original payment method.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded via bank transfer; the customer must provide bank account
  details through the order history page after the return is approved.
- Customers may instead choose store credit for an equivalent value, which is issued instantly
  once the return is approved (faster than the 5-7 day original-payment-method refund).

## Refund eligibility
- A refund is only issued for a return or a verified damaged/defective report — refunds cannot be
  requested independently of a return.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable, except when the return is due to a verified damaged or
  defective product.

## Partial refunds
- If only part of a multi-item order is returned, the refund covers only the value of the returned
  item(s), not the full order.
