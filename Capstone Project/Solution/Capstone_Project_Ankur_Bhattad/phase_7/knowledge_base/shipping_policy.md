# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
- Standard delivery: 5-7 business days from order confirmation.
- Express delivery: 2-3 business days from order confirmation, available at checkout for an
  additional fee.
- Delivery timelines are calculated in business days and exclude Sundays and public holidays.
- During peak sale periods (e.g. festive sales), standard delivery may take up to 2 additional
  business days; this will be shown at checkout when applicable.

## Shipping charges
- Orders above ₹499: free standard shipping.
- Orders ₹499 and below: a flat shipping charge of ₹49 applies.
- Express delivery has a flat charge of ₹99, regardless of order value.
- Charges are shown at checkout before payment and are non-refundable once the order has shipped,
  even if the order is later cancelled or returned.

## International shipping
- International shipping is currently available to a limited set of countries, shown at checkout
  if the delivery address qualifies.
- International orders take 10-15 business days and are subject to customs duties, which are the
  responsibility of the customer and are not included in the shipping charge.

## Delivery delays
- If a standard delivery has not arrived within 7 business days of the estimated delivery date,
  the order is eligible for a delay investigation with the carrier.
- Customers should be advised to check the tracking link first; if tracking shows no movement for
  more than 3 business days, escalate to human support for a carrier investigation.
