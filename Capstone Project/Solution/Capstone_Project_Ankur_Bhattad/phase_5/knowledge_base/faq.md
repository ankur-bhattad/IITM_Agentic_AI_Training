# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
A: The delivery address can be changed only while the order is still "Processing" (not yet
shipped). Once shipped, the address cannot be changed and the order will proceed to the original
address.

**Q: Can I track my order?**
A: A tracking link is sent by SMS/email once the order ships, and is also available on the order
history page. This assistant cannot look up live tracking status directly — customers should use
the tracking link, or contact human support if the link is not working.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and cash on delivery (COD) are accepted. COD is not
available for orders above ₹10,000 or for international addresses.

**Q: I received the wrong item. What should I do?**
A: A wrong-item delivery is treated the same as a damaged/defective report — report it within 48
hours of delivery with photos, and a free replacement or full refund will be offered.

**Q: Do you offer bulk or wholesale ordering?**
A: Bulk ordering is handled by a separate business sales team and is out of scope for this
assistant; such requests should be escalated to human support.
